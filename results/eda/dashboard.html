Critical Architectural Problems Identified & Backed by Evidence

Problem 1: Fatal Starter Evaluation Bug in baseline/eval.py (Evidence in Tab 2)
classification_report(y, pred, target_names=m.intents) omitted labels=m.intents. Because scikit-learn sorts distinct labels alphabetically by default, the reported metrics mapped account_help metrics to order_status, cancel_order to delivery_delay, etc. For example, order_status was reported with support 212 and F1=0.00, whereas its true support is 525 and true F1 is 0.65!
Problem 2: Zero Seed Examples in Hard-Coded Taxonomy (Evidence in Tab 3)
taxonomy.json has 0 examples for account_help and promo_code_issue. Centroid vectors for these two intents are identically zero, guaranteeing 0.0 cosine similarity and 0% recall in the starter model.
Problem 3: Extreme Class Imbalance & Data Starvation (Evidence in Tab 4)
The top two classes (delivery_delay and order_status) account for 29.2% of the entire corpus (4,385 and 4,383 rows), while promo_code_issue has only 325 rows (1.08%).
Problem 4: Severe Silver Label Noise in Minority Classes (Evidence in Tab 5)
5-fold cross-validated out-of-fold Logistic Regression demonstrates that label corruption heavily concentrates in minority classes: account_help suffers 75.77% OOF disagreement, and promo_code_issue suffers 63.38% disagreement.
Problem 5: Punctuation-Only and Emoji-Only Inputs (Evidence in Tab 9)
Dev and test contain inputs with zero alphanumeric tokens (e.g., '......', '???', '😀😀'). These crash text normalizers unless routed deterministically to out_of_scope per Rule 6.

Mathematical Cause & Impact:
In Scikit-Learn: classification_report(y_true, y_pred, target_names=tax_intents)
When labels is omitted, Scikit-Learn determines label indices from sorted(list(set(y_true))), which is alphabetical: ['account_help', 'cancel_order', 'change_address', ...]. But target_names was passed in taxonomy order: ['order_status', 'delivery_delay', 'cancel_order', ...].
Result: Support counts and metrics were completely scrambled across all 12 intents! As plotted above, order_status appeared completely dead (F1=0.00), when in reality it achieved F1=0.65. Conversely, promo_code_issue and account_help appeared to have F1 scores of 0.40 and 0.63, when in reality their true recall and F1 are 0.00 because they have no centroids!

System Decision Log

Decision Log: QuickCart Intent EDA


1. Single-Label vs. Multi-Label Policy

  • **Requirement**: Exactly one label per user message must be assigned.
  • **Handling Multi-Request Utterances**:
  • **Rule 1 (First Request Precedence)**: When multiple independent requests are stated (e.g. *"where is my order, and also I want a refund"*), the first actionable clause determines the label (`order_status`).
  • **Rule 2 (Mind-Change Override)**: If cue phrases signaling mind change occur (*"scratch that"*, *"actually never mind"*, *"sorry wrong thing"*, *"forget that"*), the final stated request supersedes earlier text.
  • **Rule 5 (Escalation Demotion)**: If human agent escalation is requested alongside a specific problem or order action, the specific problem/action wins. `talk_to_agent` is reserved solely for pure escalation queries.

    • 2. Text Normalization Policies

  • **Typo Handling**:
  • High-frequency spelling mistakes in domain keywords (e.g., `cancle` $\rightarrow$ `cancel`, `retund` $\rightarrow$ `refund`, `satus` $\rightarrow$ `status`, `odrer` $\rightarrow$ `order`) are mapped in `normalization_map.csv`.
  • Harmless elongation (e.g. `slowwww` $\rightarrow$ `slow`, `urgenttt` $\rightarrow$ `urgent`) is collapsed to double characters max.
  • **Abbreviations**:
  • Domain contractions (`pls`/`plz` $\rightarrow$ `please`, `eta` $\rightarrow$ `estimated_time_of_arrival`, `abt` $\rightarrow$ `about`) are expanded using `abbreviation_dictionary_draft.csv`. Units and entity identifiers (`id`, `min`) are preserved to avoid entity collisions.
  • **Emojis**:
  • Emojis account for ~2.2% of messages across splits. Emojis alone without text (`😀😀`) are routed to `out_of_scope`. In running text, emojis are kept or stripped as whitespace; they carry mild sentiment but should not override lexical intent signals.
  • **Hinglish (Code-Mixed)**:
  • Preserved as first-class tokens. High-signal Romanised Hindi words (`kahan`, `nahi`, `paisa`, `galat`, `chahiye`, `wapas`) are retained and recognized via `hinglish_lexicon.csv`. Models should use multilingual subword tokenizers or character/subword n-grams.

    • 3. Out-of-Scope Policy

  • Greetings (`"hello"`, `"good morning"`) or gratitude (`"thanks"`) alone $\rightarrow$ `out_of_scope`.
  • General knowledge questions, chit-chat, and services outside QuickCart operations (cab bookings, flights, laptops, gift cards) $\rightarrow$ `out_of_scope`.
  • In pure non-text/punctuation messages (`"???"`, `"......"`), default to `out_of_scope`.

    • Verified Empirical Findings

    Exploratory Data Analysis Findings: QuickCart Intent Dataset


    1. Summary

  • **Dataset Scale**: 30,000 train, 4,000 dev, and 3,000 test messages; 12 intent categories with zero missing or null entries.
  • **Extreme Class Imbalance**: 13.5x ratio between the head intent (`delivery_delay`: 4,385) and the tail intent (`promo_code_issue`: 325); Gini coefficient = 0.283.
  • **Taxonomy Blindspots**: 2 intents (`account_help` and `promo_code_issue`) contain zero examples in `taxonomy.json`, rendering them unmatchable by centroid-based baselines.
  • **Empirical Label Disagreement**: 5-fold cross-validated out-of-fold Logistic Regression reveals an overall label disagreement rate of **13.79%** (95% Wilson CI: [13.40%, 14.18%]), rising to 75.8% for `account_help` and 63.4% for `promo_code_issue`.
  • **High-Risk Label Queue**: 3,578 train utterances (11.9%) flagged where model prediction sharply disagrees with silver assignment with high confidence gap.
  • **Linguistic Noise Spectrum**: 22.49% of messages contain contractions/abbreviations, 14.01% contain Hinglish (Romanised Hindi), 12.17% are multi-clause rambling narratives, 6.72% exhibit spelling typos, and 2.25% include emojis.
  • **Taxonomy Overlap & Duplication**: 48 taxonomy examples occur verbatim in training queries, and contradictory examples exist between overlapping intents (`"where's my food"` in both `order_status` and `delivery_delay`).
  • **Starter Baseline Evaluation Flaw**: `baseline/eval.py` omitted `labels=m.intents` in `classification_report`, causing Scikit-Learn to sort labels alphabetically while mapping taxonomy names, yielding scrambled per-class metrics in `results/baseline_dev.json`.

    3. Critical Problems Identified

    1. **Evaluation Script Label Misalignment (High Severity)**: `baseline/eval.py` omitted `labels=m.intents` in `classification_report`, shuffling per-class metrics against alphabetical targets.

    2. **Severe Minority Class Starvation (High Severity)**: `promo_code_issue` (325) and `account_help` (388) suffer a 13.5x data deficit compared to head intents, leading to severe under-fitting in standard cross-entropy training.

    3. **Punctuation-only and Emoji-only Inputs (Medium Severity)**: Detected utterances like `'......'`, `'???'`, `'😀😀'` requiring deterministic out-of-scope routing.

    4. **Taxonomy Self-Contradiction (Medium Severity)**: Contradictory examples in `taxonomy.json` confuse unsupervised centroid matchers.
